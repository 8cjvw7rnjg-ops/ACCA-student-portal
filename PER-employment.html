What's a relevant role?

A relevant role is a job where your time is spent on activities and tasks that are accounting, finance, audit and assurance related, or in other related technical areas such as taxation, insolvency and forensics.

Experience in a relevant role could have been gained before you registered as an ACCA student, while you are studying for your exams and/or after you have completed your exams.

But remember that a practical experience supervisor from the employer where you gained the experience will need to sign-off the time and any performance objectives you claim from this employment.

Your employment

To achieve your PER you must complete 36 months supervised experience in a relevant role.

You can record your current employment details using the add employer button.  And you can also create additional employer records for previous employment. 

Adding a role

Once an employer record is created you can add a role.  You can add as many roles as you need to for each employer by using the add new role button within the employer's record. 

You can edit your records at any time by selecting the employer or role from the list on this page. 

Your practical experience supervisor

Your practical experience supervisor helps you achieve your PER by reviewing and signing off your performance objectives. They will also confirm the relevant time you are claiming towards your 36 months' experience. 

Your supervisor is linked to your role.  So once you’ve added a role to your employer record you’ll be able to add your supervisor's information to that role and send them an invitation to register.  

You won’t be able to submit anything to your supervisor for sign-off until they’ve registered, so make sure you send them an invitation as soon as you can. 

Recording your time

Your time in a relevant role is calculated automatically based on the information you enter for a role. The number of months you’ve recorded is shown beside each role. The total accumulated from all your roles is shown in the pie chart - so you can see your progress towards the 36 months’ total. Remember that all the totals displayed are always rounded down to the nearest whole month.

You’ll be able to send time to your supervisor for sign-off when you reach 36 months' experience or when you end date a role.

When should you get your time signed off?

You only need to submit a time claim to your supervisor when you leave a role or when you reach 36 months' overall experience- you will be reminded to do this.  If your circumstances stay the same then you don’t need to send regular time claims to your supervisor.

Working for an Approved Employer?

If you work for an ACCA Approved Employer who holds trainee development approval at Gold or Platinum level, then they may allow you to record your achievement of the performance objectives via a simplified route. You should always confirm this with your employer and can record this when adding your role details.

If you're unsure if you work for an Approved Employer you should check with your employer or search ACCA's Approved Employer directory.

Looking for a relevant role?

If you're not currently working or are looking to find a new relevant role you can search current vacancies via ACCA Careers.

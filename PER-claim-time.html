Claiming your time

The pie chart on the right shows the number of months that you’ve recorded in your role(s).

To make this recorded time count towards your 36 months' total you need to get it signed off by a practical experience supervisor from the employer where you gained the experience.  

Below you can see:

  • the number of months you've had signed off in each role
  • the number of months you've sent to your supervisor for sign-off in each role
  • any additional time you've got available to claim in a role- either because you've end dated a role or reached 36 months' experience.

When will I be able to send time to my supervisor for sign-off?

You’ll be able to send time to your supervisor for sign-off when you reach 36 months' experience or when you end date a role. 

Who can sign-off my time?

Your time should be signed off by the supervisor at the employer where you gained the experience. You will need to add them to your role to be able to submit it to them for approval.

Claiming your time

You need to get the time you've completed in a role signed off by your supervisor to make it count towards your 36 months' total.   

So far you've not submitted any time to your supervisor for sign-off.  Remember to submit your time to your supervisor when you leave a role or when you reach 36 months in a relevant role.  

Remember that the time should be signed off by the supervisor at the employer where you gained the time.

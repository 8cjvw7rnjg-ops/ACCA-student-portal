About this module

The Foundations in Professionalism (FIP) module is a key component of the ACCA Foundations in Accountancy (FIA) Qualification, which all students must complete as part of their FIA studies. The module is comprised of the first three units of the ACCA Ethics and Professional Skills (EPSM) module, which is part of the ACCA Qualification, and the FIP final assessment. If you successfully complete FIP and go on to study EPSM in the future, you will be given an exemption from the first three learning units of EPSM.

Why should I complete FIP?
Accountants who are professional and ethical make good accountants. It’s important for all students, irrespective of what qualification they are studying or their career aspirations, to understand what it means to work and act professionally and ethically in the workplace.

Wherever you begin your FIA journey, you will also need to complete our FIP module to gain any of the qualifications. Successful candidates will have their student record updated to recognise their achievement.

When should I complete FIP?
Online and interactive, the module helps develop your understanding of what it means to act professionally and ethically as a finance professional. The module can be completed before or after the exam component of your qualification and only needs to be completed once - great news if you plan to study further Foundations in Accountancy qualifications.

There is no time limit, the module is flexible so you can complete it at your own pace.
